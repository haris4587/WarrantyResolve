MANUFACTURER SERVICE NOTE — AURORA AUDIO ANC-7
Service bulletin: ANC7-L-CHANNEL-2026
Aurora Audio confirms that an intermittent left-channel failure is a known
manufacturing defect for a limited ANC-7 production batch. Authorized service
may replace the left driver assembly. If the driver assembly is unavailable,
the service center may approve replacement or refund under the warranty policy.
This note does not cover liquid damage, impact, or unauthorized modification.
