# WarrantyResolve Demo Warranty Policy

Product: Noise-cancelling headphones
Warranty term: 12 months from purchase date.
Covered defects: manufacturing defects, internal electronics failure, charging failure, Bluetooth failure, and active-noise-cancellation failure under normal use.
Remedy: If a covered defect is verified during the warranty period, the customer is eligible for repair, replacement, or a full refund when repair or replacement is not reasonably available.
Exclusions: accidental damage, liquid damage, unauthorized modification, misuse, cosmetic wear, loss, and theft.
Proof required: order or purchase evidence, product evidence, and a description of the defect.
This demo policy is published as immutable claim evidence for the WarrantyResolve GenLayer demonstration.
