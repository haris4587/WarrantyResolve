WARRANTY POLICY — AURORA AUDIO ANC-7
Version: 2026-01-01

Coverage
Aurora Audio covers the ANC-7 headphones for twelve months from the original
purchase date. Coverage applies to manufacturing defects that prevent normal
audio playback, charging, or Bluetooth operation when the product has been used
as intended.

Remedies
After a covered defect is verified, the customer may receive a repair, a
replacement, or a full refund of the purchase price. If an authorized repair
attempt does not restore normal operation, a replacement or full refund is the
preferred remedy. A partial refund may be used when the customer keeps a usable
product and the defect affects only a separable feature.

Evidence
The customer must provide purchase evidence and enough serial, condition, or
repair information to identify the product and the claimed failure. The seller
may provide manufacturer information, repair records, shipping records, or a
policy reference.

Exclusions
This policy does not cover damage caused by liquid, immersion, impact, crushing,
unauthorized modification, misuse, or ordinary cosmetic wear. A claim first
reported after the twelve-month period is outside warranty coverage.

Shipping and repair
Damage that occurs during a documented warranty return is reviewed separately
from the original defect. An authorized repair record is evidence of a remedy
attempt, not by itself proof that the original defect was excluded.
